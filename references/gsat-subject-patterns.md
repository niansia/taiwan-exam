# GSAT subject patterns

Use this reference with a verified Paper Profile and official item statistics. It records current high-level contracts, not a license to hard-code one administration forever.

## Evidence scope

The repository covers official GSAT item statistics from ROC 100 through 115. Treat 100–106, 107–110, and 111 onward as separate regimes. For a simulated future paper, start from the most recent verified official structure and compare at least three compatible years before declaring a feature stable.

## 國綜

Current papers recurrently open with phonetics and character-form items. Their vocabulary should be audited against current textbook/core selections and the exact administration, not generated from an arbitrary rare-character list. Do not claim that every option is from the 15/30 core classical selections unless the annual annotation proves it.

Use an explicit content budget:

- basic language/cultural knowledge versus reading-comprehension score share;
- classical versus modern-language material score share;
- textbook/core-selection anchors versus unseen external material;
- single text, paired text, thematic group, mixed response, and cross-domain material;
- lower-level retrieval versus interpretation, comparison, evaluation, and synthesis.

The 115 official feature analysis is a reference point: about 20% basic language knowledge and 80% reading, roughly 45:55 classical/modern material by score, with textbook selections around 20% and A-category core selections dominating those textbook anchors. This is one observed administration, not a universal fixed quota.

For new 國綜 papers, apply the separate user-requested editorial budget in [current-gsat-chinese-natural-form.md](current-gsat-chinese-natural-form.md): **20–25% of whole-paper points must genuinely depend on confirmed core classical selections**, not merely on any classical-language passage. Short-response subparts have a **40-character / 4-point ceiling**, with full short explanations designed for 4 points. Audit actual model-answer length, rubric demands and core-dependent points; neither a longer reading passage nor a familiar title alone satisfies these rules. Preserve the selected official major-question totals and keep 國寫 outside these limits.

## 國寫

The current contract is two reading-guided tasks: one knowledge/argument-oriented task and one affective/literary task. Both require supplied material; recent prompts may combine continuous text with images or other non-continuous material.

For the knowledge-oriented task, specify evidence extraction, conceptual framing, judgment, position, and argument constraints. For the affective task, specify the emotional or experiential lens, transformation from stimulus to personal material, narrative/reflection balance, and rhetorical control. A bare topic sentence followed by “write an essay” fails the format.

Calibrate these tasks using official scoring principles and response distributions, not objective-item P bands.

## 英文

Preserve the section sequence, passage grouping, score recipe, and non-choice tasks of the selected profile. Difficulty arises jointly from lexical frequency, syntax, cohesion, inference distance, cross-paragraph integration, option competition, passage length, genre familiarity, and time pressure.

Use [current-gsat-english-form.md](current-gsat-english-form.md) as the current-form hard contract. In particular, vocabulary and other non-reading options should compete by meaning, collocation, register, inflection, or part-of-speech behavior inside the sentence; visibly mismatched option classes are not an acceptable default. The CEEC reference list defines a practical vocabulary envelope, not a rule that every reading passage must use only its headwords.

Current papers have 50 Arabic-numbered items through the mixed section and 52 scored units when the separately numbered-by-heading translation and essay tasks are included. Do not reduce the four mixed items to two merely to force the section counts to sum to 50.

An apparently advanced topic with transparent wording is not automatically difficult. Conversely, a familiar topic can be demanding when the answer requires discourse-level inference or reconciling evidence across text and chart. Translation and composition require separate rubric calibration.

## 數學A and 數學B

Use separate populations. Math A and Math B differ in curriculum, modelling demand, representations, and intended candidate population; never create Math B by merely lowering Math A arithmetic.

Within each major section, preserve the observed entry/reset and broad progression. Current 111–115 opening single-choice medians are approximately:

- Math A questions 1–6: P = 0.78, 0.57, 0.41, 0.44, 0.35, 0.37;
- Math B questions 1–7: P = 0.55, 0.68, 0.56, 0.44, 0.58, 0.53, 0.27.

The broad correlation is negative, but individual years and adjacent items vary. Use the exact position profile and retain exceptions rather than sorting generated questions mechanically.

Mathematical competence items must make the context determine variables, assumptions, model choice, interpretation, or limitation. Decorative shopping stories wrapped around direct substitution fail the stimulus-removal test. Difficulty should come from concept connection, representation translation, modelling decisions, conditional reasoning, or proof-quality explanation—not merely large numbers or tedious calculation.

## 社會

Treat history, geography, and civics as identifiable disciplines while preserving cross-disciplinary groups. Track source type and operation: primary historical source, map, statistical chart, legal/policy text, spatial evidence, competing viewpoints, causal qualification, source criticism, and value trade-off.

Use [current-gsat-social-form.md](current-gsat-social-form.md) as the current-form hard contract. Build current-event groups from source-backed relations, not headlines; each group must remain self-contained and must tell students everything outside the syllabus that they need. Real photographs may be used when rights and provenance are recorded, but an answer-bearing grayscale reproduction must retain every necessary shape, label, boundary, texture, position, or value without relying on hue.

Current papers use substantial mixed and cross-disciplinary material, but the exact number of groups and numbered slots changes. A topical news paragraph followed by a recall question is not competence-oriented. Students must use the supplied evidence and disciplinary concepts together.

## 自然

Preserve the disciplinary coverage of earth science, physics, chemistry, and biology plus current cross-disciplinary/constructed groups. Track experiment design, variable control, graph and table reading, mechanism, model-data translation, uncertainty, and evidence-based explanation.

Balance basic concept anchors with analysis/application. Avoid making every item long; a paper needs basic items as well as competence items. For competence items, the data, setup, observation, or figure must change the reasoning path.

## Source timing and inspiration

For every historical competence item, annotate the source family, original publication date, exam date, and `lead_time_days`. Learn the timing distribution by subject and item class. When generating on a new date:

1. set an `as_of_date` and likely editorial lock date;
2. build an inspiration pool from authoritative sources available before that lock date;
3. match the historical mix of recent, recent-context, and evergreen sources;
4. require a clear curriculum bridge and rights-safe synthesis;
5. exclude sources newer than the observed minimum lead time unless the user explicitly requests rapid-response practice.

Until most historical competence items have source dates, mark source ecology `insufficient-data`. Do not advertise current-event matching based only on topic intuition.
