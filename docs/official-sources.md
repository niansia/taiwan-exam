Verified on 2026-09-03:

- 學測 subjects: 國文、英文、數學A、數學B、社會、自然. Under the 108-curriculum framework, 國文 includes 國綜 and 國寫; 社會 combines 歷史、地理、公民與社會; 自然 combines 物理、化學、生物、地球科學 and includes inquiry/practice content.
- 會考 subjects: 國文、英語、數學、社會、自然、寫作測驗. 英語 includes reading and listening; mathematics includes constructed-response items; writing is guided writing.

The CEEC reference-paper item counts are a structure baseline, not a promise that every annual administration has exactly the same count. Exact-year simulations must use a verified Paper Profile for that year.

Local official-paper ingestion keeps question PDFs, answer PDFs, and non-choice scoring principles from ROC year 100 onward. Answer-sheet and DOCX links are cataloged but not downloaded by default. ROC years 100-110 remain in the old-regime subject folders; from ROC year 111 onward, 國綜/國寫 are separate sections of 國文 and 數學A/數學B are separate subjects.
